# Mooble, Promob, and credits

Mooblender interoperates with two established product ecosystems. This page
explains how their official sites describe the products, why they exist, and
where to find the source information. The descriptions are credits, not
endorsements of this project.

## Mooble

[Mooble](https://www.mooble.com/br/) is an online 3D environment-planning and
project platform. Its official site invites people to create rooms with real
products, decorate them, draw inspiration from a project gallery, and continue
toward a store or online purchase. It also presents the platform to furniture
manufacturers, retailers, and e-commerce businesses as a way to make products
available in configurable room projects.

The interactive application is the
[Mooble Planner](https://planner.mooble.com/br/). In Mooblender, “Mooble
catalog” means the product and category information exposed by that service; it
does not mean that Mooblender owns, republishes, or licenses those products.

The current public site identifies the service with “© Mooble,” sends business
contacts to Promob-hosted pages, and links its legal terms to
[2020 Spaces](https://www.2020spaces.com/). It does not publish a separate
corporate About page from which an independent Mooble company history can be
verified. For that reason, this manual credits Mooble as the platform and brand
shown by its official site rather than inventing a separate company biography.

Official links:

- [Mooble website](https://www.mooble.com/br/)
- [Mooble Planner](https://planner.mooble.com/br/)
- [2020 Spaces](https://www.2020spaces.com/)

## Promob and Cyncly

[Promob](https://promob.com/) develops software for the furniture sector. Its
official product page describes a platform that connects design, quoting,
production, sales, and management for cabinetmakers, manufacturers, retailers,
architects, and designers. Relevant capabilities include 3D project creation,
technical product catalogs, realistic presentation, budgeting, and production
integration.

Promob's official company page identifies the business as
**Promob | A Cyncly Company**, operating under the Promob brand in Latin America
with more than 30 years of experience. [Cyncly](https://www.cyncly.com/pt-BR/)
describes its broader objective as connecting professional designers,
retailers, and manufacturers with software and product content across the path
from inspiration and design to sales and manufacturing.

Official links:

- [Promob website](https://promob.com/)
- [Promob product overview](https://promob.com/promob/)
- [About Promob and Cyncly](https://promob.com/quem-somos/)
- [Cyncly website](https://www.cyncly.com/pt-BR/)
